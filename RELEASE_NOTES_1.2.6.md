## MyMemory 1.2.6

Guidance matched a procedure only on its trigger and a decision only on its ruling, so a line that named the fix or the situation missed the card.

- A monthly procedure keeps the higher of the trigger vector, the solution vector, and the linked-event centroid.
- A monthly decision index is the entity and the ruling. Context is its own vector, so the situation words do not sit inside the standing rule.
- A daily procedure with Obstacle and Solution slots is scored from those two views. The full card is used only when the body has no slots.
- The monthly gate stays 0.31 and the daily gate stays 0.39.
